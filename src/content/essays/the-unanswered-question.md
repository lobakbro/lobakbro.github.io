The Unanswered Question is a musical work by American Composer Charles Ives in which a solo trumpet screams into the void only for the void to scream back. It is the name of a series of 6 lectures by the esteemed Leonard Bernstein who referenced this piece in his discussion of the nature and future of classical music. This blog post summarizes and expands on Bernstein's theories on linguistics and music.
